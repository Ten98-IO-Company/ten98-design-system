# Campaign and Discover: competitor evidence

Research snapshot: **19 October 2026**. Status: **research and proposed references, pending human and parent-session decisions**. No product features or shared components were implemented by this research fork.

## Scope and method

The canonical scope is the Campaign and Discover rows of [the ten-tool coverage matrix](../../UX-PATTERNS.md#coverage-for-all-ten-tools), interpreted with [the design-system decisions](../../DECISIONS.md). Public app documentation supplies compatibility constraints, not permission to expand requirements. Campaign owns publishing, media, paid advertising and delivery. Discover owns organic search research, AI-search evidence and analytics measurement.

This is a desk study of public product documentation, public UI images, review pages and adoption data. It is not a usability study with ten98 participants or authenticated hands-on acceptance of competitor applications. Each cited web source was accessed on 19 October 2026 unless a different date is explicitly recorded. Publication dates are included where useful; access date does not imply the product was tested that day.

Selection uses four independent considerations: fit to the exact task; depth and recency of independently hosted review evidence; adoption evidence with a stated population; and publicly inspectable workflow detail. We do not add incomparable figures into a synthetic league table. A high aggregate rating does not establish that a specific feature is best, accessible or available through an API. Small review samples identify candidates, not proven market leadership. Vendor product copy and vendor-reported customer counts are labelled as such. Sponsored placements and review-site AI summaries are not independent user interviews. Quotes are avoided; review observations below are paraphrased and never assigned fabricated prevalence.

The result is a **feature-specific reference shortlist**, not an exhaustive census or an endorsement to buy these products. Existing Campaign logo usage, icons, typography and login patterns remain the visual identity. Competitor references concern hierarchy, information structure and interaction. Their branding, proprietary assets and source code are not incorporated.

### AI measurements are not interchangeable

Semrush's [AI toolkit walkthrough](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1496-getting-started-with-ai-visibility-toolkit) offers visibility, competitor/topic gaps and prompt research, with estimated/benchmark metrics. Ahrefs distinguishes its broad [Brand Radar dataset](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/11064852-what-is-brand-radar-and-how-to-use-it) from custom prompts, and its [AI metrics](https://help.ahrefs.com/en/articles/15501968-ai-visibility-metrics) include search-volume-based impression estimates. [Peec's methodology](https://docs.peec.ai/intro-to-peec-ai) describes consumer-interface collection.
