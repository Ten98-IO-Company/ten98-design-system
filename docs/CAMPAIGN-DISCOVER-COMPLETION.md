## Behavior and validation boundaries

- Existing Campaign/Discover logos, icons, split login composition, native forms and controllers remain authoritative.
- New shared surfaces use scoped `t98-` rules and do not introduce an app-wide reset, theme replacement or uncontrolled-select substitution. Established compatibility imports and app-owned style reconciliation remain in place.
- Campaign retains per-account variants, immutable account currency/budget types, create-paused/readback/explicit activation, OOH provider limits, upload recovery, send receipts and unsaved-draft guards.
- Discover retains applied-query/counting units, null-versus-zero, consent/capture isolation, measurement evidence, usage accounting and paused paid operations.
- Draft, submitted, accepted, delivered and observed outcomes remain distinct. Unknown mutation outcomes require app-owned reconciliation before repeat.
- Every released archive stays immutable. App integrations pin the same new archive and keep manifest, lockfile and vendor inputs together.
- Browser fixtures and shared checks do not establish native database, server authorization, live-provider, delivery, paid-operation or production-release acceptance. App sessions own their real regression checks.
